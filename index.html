About me

I'm Alfred. I've completed a Bachelor of Business Information Technology (BBIT) at KCA University, and I work as a software developer. That mix is how I think: I enjoy the code, but I also care about why a system exists and who will use it on a Monday morning.

My biggest project is Makao, a house management system that takes rent, tenants and M-Pesa payments off paper and into one place. I designed it, built it and wrote the documentation, so I've been the developer, the analyst and the person explaining it to the lecturer.

Outside my degree I build things on my own: ebooks, the tool I made to produce them, a small browser mixtape tool, and experiments with AI automation for a YouTube channel. I speak English and German, and I'm looking for a team where I can keep building real software and learn from people better than me.

Makao House Management System

A web platform for managing rental properties, built as my final year project at KCA University. It started as student accommodation and I widened it to general residential housing once I understood the problem better.

  • M-Pesa integration so rent payments are recorded automatically
  • Full documentation: proposal, system design specification (DFDs, ERD, use case and class diagrams), work-in-progress report and implementation plan
